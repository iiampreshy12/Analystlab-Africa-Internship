# Business Intelligence & Interactive Dashboard Development
### AnalystLab Africa — Data Analytics Internship | Week 2

An executive Business Intelligence dashboard built in Power BI for a fictional national retail client, using the [Global Superstore dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/apoorvaappz/global-super-store-dataset).

## Business Problem

Senior management at the client needed a single interactive dashboard to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, and regional performance — replacing scattered, manually compiled reports with one live view of the business.

## Approach

1. **Business Understanding** — researched the role of BI and dashboards in decision-making
2. **Data Preparation** — imported and cleaned the dataset using Power Query (dates, data types, helper columns)
3. **Dashboard Development** — built a Power BI dashboard with 5 KPI cards, 8 visualisations, and 4 slicers
4. **Business Insights** — 5 key insights, 3 risks, 3 opportunities, all supported by dashboard evidence
5. **Business Recommendations** — 6 actionable recommendations for management

## Key Findings

| Driver | Finding |
|---|---|
| Market | APAC leads all markets at **$3.59M** in sales, ahead of the US ($2.30M) and EMEA ($806K) |
| Growth | Sales nearly doubled over four years — from **$2.26M** (2011) to **$4.30M** (2014) |
| Profitability | Technology is the most profitable category (**$663,740**), ahead of Office Supplies and Furniture despite comparable or lower sales |
| Risk | Tables is the only loss-making sub-category, posting a net **-$64,100** |
| Segment | The Consumer segment drives **~51%** of total sales, outperforming Corporate and Home Office combined |

## Tools

- **Microsoft Power BI** (Power Query, DAX, data modelling, interactive dashboards)
- **Excel** for initial dataset inspection

## Key Recommendations

1. Fix Tables' pricing, discounting, and shipping cost structure to eliminate its loss
2. Reallocate marketing investment toward Technology, the highest-margin category
3. Study APAC's success factors and pilot the same approach in EMEA
4. Launch a Consumer-segment loyalty programme
5. Audit discounting practices on Furniture, particularly Tables
6. Set explicit regional diversification targets for EMEA and Canada
