#Customer Behaviour & Business Performance Analysis
###AnalystLab Africa — Data Analytics Internship | Week 1

A business analytics case study investigating customer churn for a fictional telecom client, **ABC Communications Ltd**, using the [Telco Customer Churn dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn).

##Business Problem

ABC Communications Ltd engaged AnalystLab Africa Consulting to investigate why customers churn, identify the highest-risk segments, and recommend data-driven retention strategies.

##Approach

1. **Business Understanding** — framed the problem and its impact on recurring revenue
2. **Data Inspection** — checked shape, types, missing values, and duplicates
3. **Exploratory Analysis** — 3+ bar charts, 2 pie charts, 2 histograms, a box plot, and a correlation heatmap
4. **Insights** — 5 key insights, 3 business risks, 3 opportunities
5. **Recommendations** — 6 actionable retention strategies

##Key Findings

| Driver | Finding |
|---|---|
| Contract type | Month-to-month customers churn at **42.7%** vs. **2.8%** for two-year contracts (~15x gap) |
| Internet service | Fibre optic customers churn at **41.9%**, nearly 2x the company average |
| Payment method | Electronic check payers churn at **45.3%**, far above automatic payment methods |
| Tenure | Churned customers average **18.0 months**, vs. **37.6 months** for retained customers |

##Key Recommendations

1. Launch a contract-conversion campaign targeting month-to-month customers
2. Investigate the fibre optic pricing and service experience
3. Incentivise a shift away from Electronic check payments
4. Bundle Online Security and Tech Support into onboarding
5. Build a structured first-90-days retention programme
6. Develop a churn-risk scoring model for proactive outreach
